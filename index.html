A LITTLE CONTEXT

About the rates

Fex uses daily reference rates from Frankfurter. They are estimates. Your bank, card, or money changer may offer a different rate.

Rates are checked automatically while you use the app, at most once an hour during normal use. Each currency can have a different rate date. A recent check does not mean a new rate was published.

Saved rates work offline. The dates below show the data used for your current results.

Your amounts and selected list stay in this browser. Fex requests a general rate table; it does not send your amounts. The hosting and rate services receive normal network request information.
